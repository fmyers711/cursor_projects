Executive Summary

Splunk Enterprise Security Premier Edition provides a unified security analytics, automation, threat intelligence, and reporting platform that aligns directly with Cal-Secure 2.0's mission to help California state entities mature from reactive cybersecurity operations to proactive, risk-based, intelligence-driven defense. The platform supports all three Cal-Secure pillars—People, Process, and Technology—while mapping comprehensively to the six NIST CSF 2.0 functions adopted by the framework.

Splunk Enterprise Splunk Enterprise Security (SIEM) Splunk SOAR Splunk Threat Intelligence Management (TIM) Splunk UBA Risk-Based Alerting (RBA) Asset & Identity Framework Mission Control Splunk Attack Analyzer
Strategic Value: Splunk ES Premier enables California entities to centralize telemetry, prioritize by business risk, automate response at machine speed, fuse statewide threat intelligence with Cal-CSIC, and deliver executive-ready compliance and maturity reporting—supporting both large agencies operating centralized SOCs and smaller entities consuming shared security services.

Cal-Secure 2.0 People Pillar Mapping

Goal: Build a ready, capable, and diverse public sector cybersecurity workforce and be known as a place to grow a mission-driven career.

Workforce Optimization

Splunk SOAR reduces analyst fatigue by automating repetitive, low-level tasks so staff can focus on high-value investigations and threat hunting.

Skills Development

Built-in guided investigation workflows within ES help junior analysts upskill quickly. RBA dashboards teach risk aggregation reasoning. SOAR playbook editors build automation skills aligned to the NICE framework.

Career Pathways

Role-based dashboard views (Tier 1 triage → Tier 2 investigation → Tier 3 hunting → detection engineering → SOC leadership) create natural career-progression checkpoints formalizable into job ladders.

Executive Communication

ES executive dashboards and Mission Control case views translate technical events into business-risk language for agency leaders, OIS, and legislative stakeholders.

Streamlined Onboarding

Prebuilt ESCU content updates and out-of-the-box correlation rules reduce ramp-up time. SOAR playbooks encode institutional knowledge, reducing dependency on tribal knowledge.

Resource Sharing

A shared Splunk platform allows entities to exchange detection content, dashboards, and playbooks statewide. CDT/Cal-CSIC can host a central content repository for cross-entity reuse.

5-Year Rollout Roadmap Aligned to Cal-Secure 2.0

This roadmap phases Splunk ES Premier capabilities across the same multi-year horizon as Cal-Secure 2.0, progressing entities from foundational visibility to full AI-accelerated, statewide-integrated security operations.

Year 1 — Foundation & Visibility

Cal-Secure Theme: Establish baseline capabilities and common definitions across entities.

People

  • Onboard core SOC staff with Splunk ES fundamentals and notable-event triage workflows
  • Establish role-based dashboard views for Tier 1 analysts to standardize daily operations

Process

  • Deploy baseline KPI dashboards (MTTD, MTTR, alert volume) to establish statewide reporting norms
  • Map initial data sources to NIST CSF 2.0 functions (Govern, Identify, Protect) within the Organizational Profile

Technology

  • Onboard priority log sources (identity, endpoint, network, cloud) into Splunk ES
  • Deploy Splunk ES Content Updates (ESCU) for out-of-the-box detection coverage
  • Stand up Splunk SOAR with high-value playbooks (phishing triage, account lockout)
Milestone: Entities achieve baseline detection and reporting maturity, enabling participation in statewide metrics.

Year 2 — Standardization & Risk-Based Prioritization

Cal-Secure Theme: Adopt risk-based approach; streamline hiring, training, and procurement.

People

  • Launch tailored training pathways using real Splunk detections as training scenarios
  • Formalize career-ladder checkpoints (Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Detection Engineer) tied to Splunk content authorship

Process

  • Implement Risk-Based Alerting aligned with NIST SP 800-37 RMF categorization
  • Begin continuous-control validation reporting for audit and self-assessment support
  • Integrate SOAR case escalation with agency-level governance tiers

Technology

  • Expand SOAR automation for automated enrichment and triage across more use cases
  • Onboard vulnerability scanner data; correlate with asset criticality for exploit-aware patch prioritization
  • Begin threat intelligence feed integration via Splunk Intelligence Management
Milestone: Entities shift from reactive alerting to risk-prioritized operations; statewide KPI reporting becomes consistent.

Year 3 — Automation & Cross-Entity Collaboration

Cal-Secure Theme: Formalize governance, strengthen public-private collaboration, expand shared services.

People

  • Roll out Cybersecurity Communication Skills training using Splunk executive dashboards
  • Cross-train analysts on SOAR playbook development

Process

  • Formalize multi-tiered governance escalation within Mission Control, aligned to CalOES Cyber Task Force procedures
  • Package standardized Splunk dashboard/detection bundles as Cal-Secure 2.0 Toolkit for lower-maturity entities
  • Establish shared threat-intel playbooks with Cal-CSIC for bi-directional intelligence sharing

Technology

  • Expand SOAR toward continuous exposure management and adversary emulation support
  • Integrate OT/ICS telemetry for critical infrastructure entities
  • Pilot Splunk Attack Analyzer for automated malware/URL triage
Milestone: Statewide shared services model begins; smaller entities gain access to centralized detection/response support.

Year 4 — AI-Accelerated Operations & Resilience

Cal-Secure Theme: Match machine-speed threats; strengthen cyber recovery and critical infrastructure protection.

People

  • Advance senior analysts into detection engineering and threat-hunting roles with AI-assisted tools
  • Expand leadership training on translating AI/automation risk decisions to executive and legislative stakeholders

Process

  • Update KPIs and risk models to reflect AI-accelerated exploit timelines using continuously recalculated risk scores
  • Formalize joint tabletop exercises with automated SOAR playbooks simulating sector-spanning incidents

Technology

  • Deploy AI-assisted detection engineering and automated adversary emulation workflows
  • Build cyber recovery dashboards tracking backup integrity, anomalous privileged access, and restoration timelines
  • Begin cryptographic inventory visibility (certificates, TLS, key lengths) for post-quantum readiness
Milestone: Entities operate at near machine-speed detection/response; critical infrastructure and recovery resilience are measurable.

Year 5 — Statewide Maturity & Sustained Innovation

Cal-Secure Theme: Sustain progress, embed innovation, and maintain the roadmap as a living framework.

People

  • Institutionalize Splunk-based career pathways as part of statewide cybersecurity workforce planning
  • Recognize high-performing entities/analysts through statewide recognition programs tied to maturity metrics

Process

  • Achieve full statewide reporting automation with maturity dashboards feeding directly into OIS oversight
  • Continuously refine toolkits and playbooks based on lessons learned (living-document model)

Technology

  • Operate a mature, AI-driven SOC with automated remediation for well-understood threat patterns
  • Fully integrate Statewide Cybersecurity Innovation Lab testing into production rollout decisions
  • Maintain crypto-agility tracking as NIST post-quantum algorithms are finalized and adopted
Milestone: California entities operate a unified, adaptive, statewide security ecosystem consistent with Cal-Secure 2.0's long-term vision.
